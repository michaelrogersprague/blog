# About

I'm Michael Sprague, Director of Engineering at Yelp in Toronto, where I lead engineering for ads.

Before Yelp, I managed machine learning teams at Deliveroo in London. Before that, I did applied machine learning research at HERE Technologies, and I built the image-processing pipeline for an MRI-guided prostate cancer therapy at Profound Medical, which is now an FDA-cleared medical device.

I trained as a physicist. I have a B.A.Sc. in Engineering Physics from Queen's, an M.Sc. in atomic physics from the University of Toronto, and a DPhil in atomic and laser physics from Oxford, where I was a Clarendon Scholar researching quantum memories and entanglement. You can read [my DPhil thesis](/files/sprague-dphil-thesis.pdf) (PDF, 30 MB).
